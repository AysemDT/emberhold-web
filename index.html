The loop

Gather at home, build halls for gold, then sail out. Each island has a resource you can only find there and a guardian in its lair. Bring the loot back to the forge to grow stronger.

1. Gather

Strike a pine three times for 3 wood. Grey rocks take four hits and give 2 stone. Both grow back.

2. Build halls

A hall costs 8 wood, 4 stone and 10 gold. Every hall pays gold every 6 seconds and raises your rank.

3. Mend the dock

Walk to the broken pier south of your keep and press E. Repairs cost 16 wood and 10 stone.

4. Sail and conquer

Board the dock to open the sea chart. Frostmere opens at Hamlet (2 halls). Defeat each guardian to place its trophy at your keep.
